## Intended roles

- The original two Wikimedia files, Wikimedia Klam, and Openclipart 274578 are the four
  enabled face-vase stimulus families. Klam uses a source-specific top closure height for
  its face-outline derivatives; the immutable source path remains unchanged.
- Openclipart 276846/276861 remain immutable, registered provenance sources but are
  excluded as complete stimulus families.
- Queen/profile and other non-vase central figures are intentionally excluded
  from the face-vase experiment.
- Each enabled geometric family is crossed with the six ordered black/gray/white polarity
  mappings without inventing face contours.
